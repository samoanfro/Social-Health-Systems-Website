The Key Differences

Behavioral Standard

Not content distribution. Defines observable, measurable relational behaviors required across leadership and team contexts.

Earned Credentials

Belts are earned through demonstrated behavior change, not purchased. Credentials only matter if they can't be gamed.

Governed Fidelity

Platforms scale content fast. SHs scales capability with fidelity—delivery and certification are controlled to protect the standard.

Five Pillars

Connection Before Concern, Connection Currency Tools, Statements Before Questions, Relational Response to Mental Health, Relationship-Based Culture.

9-Module Curriculum

Sequential installation of observable capabilities across 6 phases. Every module assessed against a defined rubric at a defined tier.

5-Tier Pathway

Entry → Practitioner → Specialist → Coach → Master. Multi-year arc with prerequisites, observed role-plays, and portfolio evidence.
